# Remote Working

Staff may work away from the office up to three days a week by default. Fully remote
arrangements are possible and need director approval.

Staff working remotely receive an allowance of eighty dollars a month towards internet and
electricity. It is paid with salary and does not need receipts.

A one off budget of five hundred dollars is available for a desk and chair. Anything above
that follows the normal approval thresholds.

Core hours are ten in the morning to four in the afternoon in the office's time zone.
Outside core hours staff manage their own schedule.

Staff working from another country for more than thirty days a year must tell the people
team first, because of tax and right to work rules.
