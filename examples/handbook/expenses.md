# Expenses

Staff can claim back money they spend on work. Claims are submitted in the expenses tool
and paid with the following month's salary.

Every claim needs an itemised receipt. A card statement is not a receipt because it does
not show what was bought.

Travel by train or bus is reimbursed in full at standard class. Taxis are reimbursed when
public transport is not running or when carrying equipment.

Meals while travelling overnight are reimbursed up to forty dollars a day.

Claims must be submitted within sixty days of the spend. The finance team rejects anything
older without a written explanation from a manager.

Some categories of spend cannot be claimed at all. Those are listed separately.
