# Sick Leave

Staff who are unwell should tell their manager before the start of their working day.

No doctor's note is needed for the first three consecutive days. From the fourth day
onwards a note is required and should be sent to the people team.

Sick days are paid in full for the first twenty working days in any twelve month period.
Beyond that, statutory rates apply.

Sick leave does not come out of the annual holiday allowance.

Staff who fall ill during a booked holiday can ask for those days to be returned to their
holiday balance, with a doctor's note covering the dates.
