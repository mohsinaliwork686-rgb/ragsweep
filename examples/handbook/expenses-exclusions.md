# Expenses That Cannot Be Claimed

The following are never reimbursed, whatever the amount and whoever approves them.

Alcohol is not reimbursed, including with a meal that is otherwise claimable.

Fines and penalties are not reimbursed. This includes parking tickets, speeding fines and
late filing penalties, even when incurred while working.

Personal grooming, clothing and dry cleaning are not reimbursed, including clothing bought
specifically for a client visit.

Flight upgrades are not reimbursed. Staff may upgrade at their own cost.

Home broadband is not reimbursed as an expense. Remote staff receive a separate monthly
allowance instead, which is covered by the remote working policy.

Gifts for colleagues are not reimbursed. Gifts for customers need director sign-off first.
