# Data Retention

Customer order records are kept for seven years, because tax law requires it.

Support conversations are kept for two years from the last message, then deleted
automatically.

Recordings of customer calls are kept for ninety days unless they are attached to an open
complaint.

Job applications from candidates we did not hire are kept for six months, then deleted.

Staff records are kept for six years after someone leaves.

A customer can ask for their personal data to be deleted. The request goes to the security
team, who have thirty days to act on it, and must keep anything tax law requires us to
keep.
