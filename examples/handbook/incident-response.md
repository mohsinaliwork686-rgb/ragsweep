# Incident Response

An incident is anything that affects customers, data or the availability of a service.

Severity one means customers are affected right now and there is no workaround. It is
escalated immediately to the on call engineer and the duty director, at any hour.

Severity two means a significant problem with a workaround in place. It is handled during
working hours and reviewed the next working day.

Severity three means a minor problem with no customer impact. It goes into the normal
backlog.

The first responder's job is to stabilise the service, not to find the root cause. Finding
the cause happens afterwards.

Every severity one and severity two incident gets a written review within five working
days. Reviews name systems and decisions, never individuals.
