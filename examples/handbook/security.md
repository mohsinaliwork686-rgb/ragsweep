# Security

Every account must use multi factor authentication. The hardware key issued at onboarding
is the preferred second factor; an authenticator app is acceptable where a key will not
work.

Passwords must be at least sixteen characters. We do not force regular rotation, because
forced rotation leads to weaker passwords, but any password suspected of being exposed
must be changed immediately.

Company laptops must have full disk encryption switched on. IT checks this monthly and
will lock a device that fails the check.

Work data must not be copied to personal cloud storage or personal email.

Anyone who thinks something has gone wrong, however small, should report it. Reporting
quickly is never penalised, including when the person reporting caused the problem.
