# Annual Leave

Every full time member of staff gets twenty five days of paid holiday a year, plus public
holidays.

Requests must be submitted at least two weeks before the first day off. Shorter notice is
at the manager's discretion and is not guaranteed.

Unused days do not carry over into the next year. The balance resets on the first of
January and anything left is lost.

No more than two people in the same team can be away at the same time. The tool blocks a
third request automatically.

Part time staff get a pro rata amount based on the days they work.

Time off because of illness is handled under a different policy and does not come out of
this allowance.
