# Refunds

A refund returns money to the customer for an order that has already been paid for.

Once a refund is approved, the finance team processes it within five business days. The
money is returned to the original payment method. We do not issue store credit in place
of a refund unless the customer asks for it in writing.

Customers must raise a refund request within thirty days of the delivery date. Requests
raised after that window are declined automatically by the billing system and need a
manager to reopen them.

Refunds above a certain value need a sign-off before finance will release them. The
thresholds and who can approve them are listed in the approvals policy.

Partial refunds are allowed. An agent can refund part of an order without cancelling the
rest of it.
