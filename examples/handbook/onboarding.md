# Onboarding

New starters are sent a laptop and a security key to arrive the working day before they
begin.

Accounts are created by the IT team in advance. A new starter should be able to sign in to
email, chat and the ticketing system on their first morning.

The first week is deliberately light. It covers a tour of the systems, an introduction to
the team, and the mandatory security training.

Every new starter is paired with a buddy from a different team for their first month. The
buddy is not their manager and is there for questions people feel awkward asking a manager.

Probation lasts three months and ends with a written review.
