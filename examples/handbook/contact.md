# Who To Contact

The people team handles pay, holiday, sick leave, parental leave and anything about a
contract. Reach them in the people channel.

The IT team handles laptops, accounts, access and anything that will not sign in. Raise a
ticket rather than messaging an individual.

The finance team handles expense claims, refunds to customers and supplier invoices.

The security team handles anything suspicious: a strange email, a lost laptop, a password
that may have been exposed.

For anything affecting customers right now, follow the incident response policy rather
than contacting a team directly.
