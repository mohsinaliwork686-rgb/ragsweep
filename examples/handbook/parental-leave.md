# Parental Leave

Staff who are having or adopting a child are entitled to parental leave regardless of
gender or whether they are the primary carer.

The primary carer gets twenty six weeks at full pay, followed by up to twenty six weeks
unpaid.

The secondary carer gets eight weeks at full pay, which can be taken in up to three
separate blocks within the first year.

Notice should be given at least fifteen weeks before the expected date where possible.

Leave can start up to four weeks before the expected date. It must start no later than the
date itself.

Staff keep their holiday allowance while on parental leave and it continues to build up.
