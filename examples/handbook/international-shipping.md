# International Shipping

Orders shipped outside the country take up to fourteen working days to arrive. This is
much longer than domestic delivery because of customs processing.

Free delivery does not apply to international orders. International postage is charged by
weight and is calculated at checkout.

Customers are responsible for any import duties and local taxes charged when the parcel
enters their country. We cannot predict these amounts and we do not refund them.

We do not currently ship to a small number of countries where our carrier has no service.
The checkout will say so before payment is taken.

Express delivery is not available on international orders.
