# Approvals

Spending and money movements above set values need a second person to agree before they
go ahead. This applies to refunds, purchases and credits alike.

Amounts up to five hundred dollars can be approved by the agent handling the case, with
no second signature.

Amounts above five hundred dollars and up to five thousand dollars need a team manager to
sign off.

Amounts above five thousand dollars need a department director to sign off. Directors
cannot delegate this to a manager.

The person who raises a request can never approve their own request, regardless of the
amount or their seniority.

Every sign-off is recorded against the ticket with the approver's name and a timestamp.
