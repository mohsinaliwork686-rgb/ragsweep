# Shipping

Standard delivery is free on orders over fifty dollars. Orders below that value are
charged a flat delivery fee of four dollars ninety-five.

Standard delivery arrives in two to four working days.

Express delivery arrives the next working day when the order is placed before two in the
afternoon. Express costs twelve dollars regardless of order value, including on orders
that would otherwise ship free.

We do not deliver on weekends or public holidays. An order placed on Friday afternoon
with express delivery arrives on Monday.

Orders going outside the country follow a separate policy.
