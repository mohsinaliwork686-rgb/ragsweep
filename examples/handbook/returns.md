# Returns

A return is the physical process of sending goods back to our warehouse. It is separate
from a refund, which is the movement of money.

Customers have fourteen days from delivery to start a return. That is a shorter window
than the thirty days allowed for a refund request, because the goods have to physically
reach us.

Every return needs an RMA number before it is shipped. Parcels that arrive without an RMA
number are held at the warehouse and cannot be matched to an order.

Goods must come back unused and in the original packaging. Items that arrive damaged or
missing parts are assessed by the warehouse team, who decide whether to accept them.

Return postage is paid by the customer unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong
product.
